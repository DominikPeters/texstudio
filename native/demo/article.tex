\section{Coefficients}
Let $f$ be a $2\pi$-periodic function that is integrable on $[-\pi,\pi]$.
Its Fourier coefficients are
\[
c_n = \frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} f(x)\, e^{-inx}\,dx, \qquad n \in \mathbb{Z}.
\]

\section{Convergence}
The partial sums $S_N f$ need not converge pointwise for every
continuous $f$, but they converge in $L^2$.
